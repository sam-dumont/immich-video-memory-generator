# Memory types and their parameters

The [Memory page](./memory.mdx) asks what the memory is about and how long it runs. Its **Memory type** select offers exactly what `generate --memory-type` accepts — Year in Review, Season, Person Spotlight, Multi-Person, Monthly Highlights, On This Day, Album, Trip, Holiday, Surprise me — plus **Custom date range**. This page lists what each type asks for once you pick it.

The Immich connection panel and the pool switches that used to open the wizard's first step live under **Advanced** on the Memory page, and **Cut** replaces the wizard's "Next: Review Clips".

## What each type asks for

Each type sets the date range and a default target duration, then shows the parameters it needs:

| Type | Parameters shown | Default target |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| Year in Review | Year, Only with | 10 min |
| Season | Year, Season (spring/summer/autumn/winter), Hemisphere (north/south), Only with | 2 min 15 s |
| Person Spotlight | Year (or "All Time"), Person, checkbox "Birthday to birthday" | 10 min |
| Multi-Person | Year, People (select 2+), Together (AND) / Any of (OR) | 10 min |
| Monthly Highlights | Year, Month, Only with | 1 min |
| On This Day | Only with (otherwise uses today's date across previous years) | 45 s |
| Holiday | Holiday, Most recent year, Years to span (2–10), Only with | 1 min |
| Trip | Year, then "Select a trip" from the trips detected in your GPS data | auto: 30 s + 10 s per active day, bounded 60–300 s |
| Album | "Album" from your Immich albums | auto: 4 s per item in the album, bounded 30 s–10 min |
| Surprise me | "Pick a day" from the days [`discover-days`](../cli/discover-days.md) found | auto: 30 s + 6 s per active hour, bounded 60–180 s |
| Custom date range | date range tabs and person filter (below) | about 10 min per year of range |

### Memory types that span several years

**On This Day** and **Holiday** do not cover one continuous period. Each builds a
**separate window per year** and the page fetches every one of them, so a Holiday
memory spanning five years queries five windows around that holiday rather than
the five years between them.

**Holiday** takes any of the ten holidays the pipeline resolves — New Year,
Valentine's Day, Easter, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving,
Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve — plus the most recent year to include
and how many years to reach back. Its title is the occasion ("Christmas"),
subtitled "Through the Years".

The types also drive the output filename and title screen. A Multi-Person memory with Riley and Bob for March 2025 produces `riley_bob_march_2025_memories.mp4` with "Riley & Bob" as the title subtitle.

### Surprise me

The other types ask you what the memory is about. This one asks the
library. `immich-memories discover-days` walks the years a month at a time and
asks the local model which days stand out on their own — a day that ran long,
shot a lot, and was not a holiday or part of a trip — and writes what it found
to `~/.immich-memories/special-days.json`. The type offers those days and
nothing else.

The list is ordered anniversaries first, labelled *"10 years ago — A long
evening out"*, then every other catalogued day by how round its anniversary is
and how recent it is. This is deliberately not what
[automation](../cli/auto.md) does: a scheduled run only proposes a day on its
anniversary, because it arrives unannounced and the round number is what earns
the interruption. You picked the type, so you get the whole catalogue.

Choosing a day scopes the memory to **the hours it happened in** rather than to
midnight-to-midnight, when the scan recorded a window for it, and the day's own
title and subtitle carry through to the title screen on the options page — the
catalogue named it from that day's photos, so the title LLM is not asked for a
second opinion. A special day is one occasion, so it gets a single opening card
and no month dividers.

If there is no catalogue yet, the page says which file it looked for and which
command builds one, and the brief stays incomplete. It will not offer a random day.

## Person selection

Every person dropdown is populated from Immich's face recognition data, so only
named faces are offered.

Two types are *about* people and collect them as part of the memory: **Person
Spotlight** takes one person, and **Multi-Person** takes 2 or more. Multi-Person
also has a **Together (AND) / Any of (OR)** toggle. Together requires every
chosen person in each asset; Any of builds the union of their tagged assets.

Every other type that covers a period — Year in Review, Season, Monthly
Highlights, On This Day, Holiday — carries an optional **Only with** picker
instead. Leave it empty and the memory covers everyone. Name one person and it
becomes that person's version of the memory. Name several and only moments with
**all** of them are used, which is exactly what repeating `--person` does on the
CLI:

```bash
immich-memories generate --memory-type year_in_review --year 2025 --person "Riley" --person "Bob"
```

Three types carry no person filter, each for its own reason:

- **Album** is its own pool — the album decides what is in the memory, so
  `--from-album` rejects `--person` too.
- **Trip** takes its window whole on both surfaces. On the CLI `--person`
  narrows trip *detection* — whose GPS trail is scanned — and the trip that
  comes back is built from everything shot in that window.
- **Surprise me** is the occasion rather than its guest list.

**Custom date range** keeps its own single-person **Person** filter under "Person
Filter", defaulting to **All people** — it belongs to the custom range rather than
to a preset.

## Custom date range

Choosing **Custom date range** opens three tabs:

- **Year**: pick a year, then choose **Calendar Year** or **From Birthday** (a birthday-to-birthday range, with a Birthday date field). Good for birthday compilations.
- **Duration**: a number (1–24) plus a unit (Months/Years) and a **Starting from** date.
- **Custom Range**: **Start date** and **End date**.

The resulting range and its length in days are shown under the tabs. With Auto duration on, the target follows the range at roughly 10 minutes per year (so a month is 1 minute, a half year 5); an override typed on the Memory page's duration line stands.

## Duration

The target is the complete video, including titles and transition overlap. Types set it (table above); the Memory page's duration line shows the resolved value and takes an exact override. For Trip memories Auto recomputes it from the media once loaded: 30 seconds plus 10 seconds per active day, bounded to 60–300 seconds when the selected media is dense enough, then capped by usable excerpts (a sparse trip can end up under a minute).

| Range | Auto-target | Notes |
|-------|------------|-------|
| 3 active days (trip) | 1 min | Dense-media Auto floor |
| 1 week (trip) | 1 min 40 sec | Before the media-capacity check |
| 12 active days (trip) | 2 min 30 sec | Before the media-capacity check |
| 1 month (Monthly Highlights or Custom) | 1 min | |
| 3 months (Season) | 2 min 15 s | Preset default |
| 1 year (Year in Review or Custom) | 10 min | |

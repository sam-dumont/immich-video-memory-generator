# Why Immich Memories?

## How it started

End of December 2025. My son's birthday was coming up and I wanted to make a yearly recap of his best moments. I had hundreds of clips, all safely stored after I moved my library to Immich.

I opened Premiere, imported 40 clips, started dragging them onto a timeline. Added a few crossfades. Realized I needed music. Two hours later I had 90 seconds of mediocre output and I still hadn't gone through the other 360 clips.

Never finished it.

The problem isn't storage (Immich handles that) or finding clips (search works). The problem is the 4-8 hours between "I have 400 clips" and "here's a 3-minute video I'd actually show my family."

## What I built instead

A tool that does the boring part for me. It connects to Immich (read-only by default; `--upload-to-immich` is the one thing that writes back), looks at a time period I pick, and figures out which clips are worth keeping. Nothing is scored. A small vision model writes one description per picture, once. A text model then reads the whole period, decides what the stories in it are, weighs them against each other and hands each story the pictures it earns, with a written reason for every one. Then it assembles the result into a video with title screens, transitions, and music.

The key: I still get to review what it picked and remove anything I don't want. But I'm reviewing 30 pre-selected clips instead of scrolling through 400. Every feature in it traces back to removing one manual step, which is why there is an editor instead of a scorer, why Live Photos get stitched, why music is generated, and why a trip opens on a map.

If you're running Immich, you already made the decision to own your data. This gives you something to do with it.

## Limitations

- **No mobile app.** Web UI and CLI. Works in mobile browsers but there's no native app.
- **Not instant, and the wait is reading rather than encoding.** The first cut over a period captions and measures every picture in it and has the text model read the period; both are cached, so a second cut over the same period is mostly the render. Batch process.
- **Not a video editor.** Select/deselect clips and adjust segments, but no timeline.
- **Requires Immich.** Companion tool, not a standalone photo manager.

# The web UI

Reader: newcomer. Every page and button, in the order you meet them.

`/` is one page with three states: the **brief**, the **cut** in progress, and the **storyboard**
the cut produced. It is `immich-memories generate` with buttons: one memory type, its parameters,
one duration, one button. The sidebar holds **Memory**, **Suggestions**, **Runs**, **Media pool**
and **Settings**. Options and Export open from the cut itself.

Screenshots on this page come from a hermetic run over the CC0 fixture library
(`tests/e2e/fixtures/library`, credits in its `CREDITS.md`), never a real library.

## The brief

The brief appears once Immich answers. With credentials in `config.yaml` or the environment the page
connects on its own; otherwise only the connection panel shows until **Test Connection** succeeds.

The API key field always loads empty: the saved key stays on the server, and an empty field keeps
it. Change the server URL and you type the key again, because a saved key only goes to the server it
was saved for.

**Memory type** offers what `generate --memory-type` accepts, plus **Custom date range**
(`--start`/`--end` on the CLI). **Duration** is one line: with **Auto duration** on it shows the
type's answer ("Auto · 1m 00s" for a month); off, you type an exact target. The target is the whole
video, titles included.

The cut fits Auto to the pool when it starts, with the same rule as `generate` without
`--duration`: a film never runs longer than its days can fill. A month with three photographed days
gets about 20 s. After the cut the line says what set the length ("Duration 20s, set by the
material: 3 photographed days"). The rule: [How long a film runs](./memory-types.mdx#how-long-a-film-runs).

| Type | Parameters shown | Default target |
|------|------------------|----------------|
| Year in Review | Year, Only with | 10 min |
| Season | Year, Season, Hemisphere, Only with | 2 min 15 s |
| Person Spotlight | Year (or "All Time"), Person, **Birthday to birthday** | 10 min |
| Multi-Person | Year, People (2 or more), Together / Any of these people | 10 min |
| Monthly Highlights | Year, Month, Only with | 1 min |
| On This Day | Only with | 45 s |
| Album | Album, from your Immich albums | 30 s + 10 s an active day, 60 to 300 s |
| Trip | Year, then **Select a trip** from the trips found in your GPS | 30 s + 10 s an active day, 60 to 300 s |
| Holiday | Holiday, Most recent year, Years to span (2 to 10), Only with | 1 min |
| Surprise me | **Pick a day** from the `discover-days` catalogue | 30 s + 6 s an active hour, 60 to 180 s |
| Custom date range | date range tabs, person filter | about 10 min per year of range |

What each type covers is on [Memory types](./memory-types.mdx). Three carry no person filter: Album
(the album decides), Trip (its window is taken whole) and Surprise me (the occasion, not its guest
list). **Surprise me** offers only days in `~/.immich-memories/special-days.json`, anniversaries
first; with no catalogue it says which command builds one rather than offering a random day.

**Custom date range** has three tabs: **Year** (then **Calendar Year** or **From Birthday**),
**Duration** (1 to 24 months or years from a start date) and **Custom Range**.

### People

Every person picker is filled from Immich's face recognition, so only named faces appear. **Person
Spotlight** takes one, **Multi-Person** two or more, and every other dated type has an optional
**Only with** picker.

With two names or more, **Together** keeps pictures holding everyone (repeating `--person`) and **Any
of these people** keeps pictures holding one of them (`--person-match or`). **Advanced people
condition** takes quoted names with AND, OR and parentheses, like `--people-expression`. It opens by
itself when a condition is set, so a filter never narrows a memory with nothing on screen saying so.

## Advanced

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-brief-advanced" alt="Advanced: the Immich connection panel, the pool switches and the media pool button" />

**Advanced**, at the bottom of the brief, holds the **Immich connection** panel, four pool switches
(**Include Photos**, **Include Live Photos**, **Accept Forwarded Media**, **HDR clips only**) and
**Open the media pool**. Read [Saving config](#saving-config-from-the-memory-page) before pressing
**Save Config**: it writes the whole configuration.

### The media pool

The pool is everything the brief found, in one chronological grid with an **Include** checkbox on
each, twenty to a page. Everything starts checked. Untick what may never be used: the pocket
recording, the forwarded screenshot. Twins and bursts are the editor's call; nothing is pre-unticked.

After a cut the same checkboxes show the cut. Untick a picture the editor kept and **Cut again**
leaves it out. Tick one it dropped and **Cut again** keeps it, in its own story, at its capture time,
without re-arguing the rest. The only thing that outranks your tick is the family-viewing gate, and
the run's `owner-required-after-audience` record names what it refused. **Start over** forgets the
cut. More ways to overrule the editor: [Overrule it](../how-it-chooses/overrule-it.md).

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-pool-outcomes" alt="The pool shows the last cut's outcome and reason below each picture" />

Each picture also shows its outcome from the last cut: **In the cut at** a timecode with the reason,
or **Left out at** the named pass with the reason. `immich-memories runs why ASSET_ID --run RUN_ID`
reads the same evidence in a terminal.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-pool-outcomes-grid" alt="The compact grid: the same outcome line under every thumbnail" />

**Trim the video clips**, after a cut, opens the excerpt editor: one row per video, with **Include in
compilation**, a **Select range** slider (plus **First 5s**, **Middle 5s**, **Last 5s**, **Full
clip**, **Preview**) and **Rotation**. Edits go back into the cut and the length updates as you trim.

## Cut

**Cut** loads the pool for the brief and runs the editor over it. There is no review step in between:
everything the brief found is eligible. To keep something out first, open the media pool, untick it,
and cut from there.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-cutting" alt="The cut in progress: one row per phase, the editor's current stage under the active one" />

Five rows (**Finding media**, **Loading thumbnails**, **Reading the pictures**, **Editing**, **Done**)
show the current stage, the elapsed time and **Cancel**, which stops after the stage in progress. On
a library that has never been read, *Reading dates, places and people* is the long one; a later cut
over the same pictures reuses what it banked. The stages are explained on
[From library to film](../how-it-chooses/overview.md).

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-cutting-estimate" alt="A preparation pass showing its count and estimated time left in this stage" />

A stage that counts its work shows a bar and an estimate measured from its own finished work.

One session runs one cut at a time, and the run belongs to the session, not the tab. Reload mid-cut
and you land on the same rows. A cut that was cancelled, failed or interrupted offers **Cut again**.

With a reader configured ([optional](../better/overview.md)), a reader that stops answering shows on
the Editing row at once (*Waiting for the reader at omlx.local:9999: connection dropped, retry 1 of
3*): a server that is restarting survives, one that is off is named within seconds.

## The storyboard, then the story

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-story" alt="The storyboard: the thesis, then one shot per picture in capture order with its day, story and running timecode" />

The **Storyboard** is the cut in the order the video plays it: a one-paragraph **thesis** on top,
then one row per picture with its timecode, capture day, seconds on screen, story and the editor's
one-line reason. Read the day labels down the list: six pictures under one story across three days
is a grouping that went loose, and this is where you see it.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-story-ranked" alt="The story tab: stories in weight order, each with its pictures and their reasons" />

The **Story** tab is the same cut the way the editor weighed it. A duration line first (seconds
selected, seconds available, near target or short), then the stories heaviest first (**Main story**,
**Important**, **Supporting**, **Small moment**). **Details** shows the editor's own words: the
weight (`dominant`, `major`, `minor`, `glimpse`) and each picture's standing (`remarkable`, `maybe`).
The [Glossary](../how-it-chooses/glossary.md) defines them.

Four buttons below: **Export**, **Review the pool**, **Cut again** and **Change the brief**.

## Export

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-export" alt="Export: summary, output filename, upload switch" />

**Preview & Export** shows the **Film length** (titles included, with `≈` until the file exists), the
**Output filename** (`everyone_jan-apr_2025_memories.mp4`), the folder (`output.directory`,
`~/Videos/Memories` by default, one subfolder per run) and **Upload after generation** with an
**Album name**, off by default. Upload is the one write this app makes to Immich: the film, tagged
`immich-memories/generated`, into that album.

**Generate Video** starts the render, which keeps going on the server if the tab reloads. There is no
download button: open the path shown, or find the film in the album.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-options" alt="Generation Options: output settings, title, music" />

**Back to Generation Options** changes how the cut is rendered, never the cut. **Resolution** and
**Output Format** default to `output.codec` and `output.format`. **Advanced options** adds
**Orientation** (Auto follows the majority of the kept clips), **Scaling Mode**, **Transition Style**,
the date and place captions, **Keep intermediate files** and **Photo duration**. Clips are never
cropped: a mismatched aspect gets a blurred backdrop or bars
([Ken Burns and framing](./photos-and-live-photos.md#ken-burns-and-where-the-pan-lands)).

**Title** and **Subtitle** come pre-filled, with a **Language** select and **Regenerate**.
**Background music** offers **None** and **Upload file**; **Bundled** appears when the music package
is installed, **AI Generated** only when a music generator is enabled. See
[Titles, maps and music](./titles-maps-music.md).

Hardware acceleration is not shown here: the app picks it at encode time, and `immich-memories hardware`
says what your machine offers.

## Suggestions

<ThemedScreenshot name="suggestions" alt="Suggested memories with their dates, reasons and actions" />

**Suggestions** shows what automation would make next, with the same ranking as
`immich-memories auto suggest`: reason, dates, picture count and category per card. **Candidate key**
shows the key `auto run --candidate` accepts, **Check eligibility** runs the checks without a render,
and **Run this suggestion** renders on the server. Running re-checks cooldown and repetition rules
first. **Why other suggestions were skipped** names the rule behind each rejection. More on
[Automate it](./automate.md).

## Runs

<ThemedScreenshot name="runs" alt="Run history with status, dates and links to each run" />

**Runs** lists manual and automatic renders from the same database as `immich-memories runs list`,
failures included. A run shows its output path, upload status, warnings and phase timings. **Read the
cut** shows the saved storyboard (`runs story` on the CLI). **Download child output** fetches the full
output of an automatic run, with credentials removed.

## People: confirming who's who

`/settings/people` edits `~/.immich-memories/people.yaml`, the same file
[`immich-memories people`](./cli/prepare.md) writes. The scan reads each named person's picture count
and month curve from Immich, never a pixel. This page is what turns "people in the picture" into
"your family" for the editor: the family seat and big stories read it
([Teach it your family](../get-started/who-is-who.md)).

A fresh install shows "No people file yet". **Rescan the library** writes the file and draws the
roster. What the scan infers goes under `inferred:` and is recomputed each scan; what you confirm
goes under `confirmed:` and a scan never touches it.

The roster shows 20 people a page, inner circle first, with **Find a name** above. Each card has the
tier the scan gave and why, the birth date from Immich (edit it in Immich) and a face crop. Three
things are yours: **Role** (pick a suggestion or type your own), **Notes**, and **Relationships**
(check confirms, cross rejects, pressing again takes it back). **Add relationship** records one the
scan missed; **Add someone not in Immich** adds a person with no face record.

**Curation** lists what only Immich can fix: two records with one name, or one person split across
records, each linked to the record in Immich.

## Config: what is actually running

`/settings/config` is a **read-only view** of the live configuration: `config.yaml` after environment
overrides and after any preset filled in the keys you did not set. It answers "is the default the
docs mention what this install is using?". **Reload from Disk** re-reads the file after a hand edit.
Every key is in the [configuration reference](../reference/config-reference.md).

Any key named `api_key`, `api_keys`, `caption_api_key`, `client_secret`, `password`, `secret`,
`token`, `trigger_token` or `urls` shows as `***`, masked on the server, so a screenshot of this page
leaks nothing.

### Saving config from the Memory page

**Save Config**, in the connection panel under **Advanced** on the Memory page, writes the Immich URL
and key, but it saves the **whole configuration**, so every value becomes explicitly set. A preset
only fills what you have not chosen, so after a save it owns nothing: delete keys from `config.yaml`
by hand to hand them back. Two exceptions: `server.host` is left out unless you set it, and
credentials from environment variables stay in the environment.

## Cache: what is on disk

`/settings/cache` lists four caches with their size and a **Clear** button each.

| Cache | Holds | Cost of clearing |
|-------|-------|------------------|
| **Analysis** | a table no cut reads | nothing |
| **Video** | downloaded source videos | re-download from Immich; evicted on its own anyway |
| **Thumbnail** | Immich previews, read back for pixel facts, heads, contact sheets and the grid | re-fetched on demand |
| **Preview** | the `.mp4` previews the review page plays | regenerated on the next preview |

The expensive thing on disk is not behind those buttons. The editor's bank, `annotations.sqlite`
under the cache directory, holds every fact it read about your pictures. **Clear all caches** leaves
it alone. Delete it and the next cut reads your library from scratch. More on
[Health, logs and caches](../run/maintenance/health-logs-cache.md).

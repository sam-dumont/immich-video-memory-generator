# Why Immich Memories?

## How it started

End of December 2025. My son's birthday was coming up and I wanted to make a yearly recap of his best moments. I had hundreds of clips, all safely stored after I moved my library to Immich.

I opened Premiere, imported 40 clips, started dragging them onto a timeline. Added a few crossfades. Realized I needed music. Two hours later I had 90 seconds of mediocre output and I still hadn't gone through the other 360 clips.

Never finished it.

The problem isn't storage (Immich handles that) or finding clips (search works). The problem is the 4-8 hours between "I have 400 clips" and "here's a 3-minute video I'd actually show my family."

## What I built instead

A tool that connects to Immich, reads a period I choose, and builds a video with titles,
transitions and music. I can review the selection before rendering and exclude pictures I do
not want.

The model reader groups the period into stories and writes a reason for each selected picture.
The rules reader works without that model, using dates, places, favourites, people and available
image facts. [Running modes](../deploy/running-modes.md) explains the tradeoffs.

## What it handles

- Person filters using Immich's face tags, and face-aware framing for photos.
- Duplicate grouping, photos, video and Live Photo motion in the same cut.
- A music file, bundled tracks or optional generated music, with ducking under source audio.
- Titles and trip maps.
- Daily automation through `auto run`, which chooses an eligible memory or retries a delivery.

## Limitations

- **Requires Immich.** It does not manage a standalone photo library.
- **Web UI and CLI.** There is no native mobile app or editing timeline.
- **Preparation takes time.** Wider periods need more previews and image analysis. Compatible
  saved facts are reused, but changing models or settings can require more work.
- **Selection needs review.** It can miss a moment, keep a poor picture or misclassify sensitive
  material. This is a beta product.

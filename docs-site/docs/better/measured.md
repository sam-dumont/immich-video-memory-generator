The next measurement is under way, on one version of the code, in two halves:

- **A plain NAS**, no model: four months, a year, and a person film across twenty years, cold and warm.
- **The model tier** on the same films: the reader for prose and the polish only, the Laya
  pre-screen on, no film-time pixels.

Each gets the same columns: host, tier, cold month, warm month, cold year, model calls, EUR at
list price, and shots in the film. Until it lands, this page has no table, on purpose: older
numbers measured a pipeline that no longer exists (the reader read whole periods and looked at
pictures at film time), and they would size a setup wrong.

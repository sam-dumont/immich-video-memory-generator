# Immich Memories

Turns your self-hosted [Immich](https://immich.app) library into edited memory videos: a year
in review, a month, a trip with its map, one person across the years. It reads the period as a
story, keeps the pictures that carry it, always in chronological order, and writes down why
every picture is in the cut.

:::caution Beta, under heavy rework, not stable
The selection engine was replaced by a story-driven editor in September 2026, and `main` and the
`latest` Docker tag move with the work that follows. Expect runs that fail, photos that go
missing, and options that move between releases. Try it on one small album first, and file what
breaks.
:::

## What comes out

- A cut with a reason: every picture in it carries the one line that says why it is there, and
  the storyboard shows the video in the order it plays before you render it.
- Title screens, month dividers, and for a trip an animated satellite map from home to where you went.
- Music: your own file, one of 28 bundled tracks, or a generated one (ACE-Step or MusicGen).
- Photos, videos and Live Photos in one cut, HDR kept end to end.

## What it talks to

Your Immich server, over your LAN. No cloud, no account, no telemetry. Three ways to run it:

| | What you need | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Rules only | the app alone; a 4-core NAS is enough | all ten memory types from dates, places, favourites and people; simpler cuts |
| Rules plus classifiers | about 500 MB of pinned ONNX models | the sensitive-content and document detectors, so the family-viewing gate has evidence |
| Model reader | a machine that holds a vision model with a 32k context, or a hosted one | the full editor: it reads the period as a story and argues for each picture |

The caption server and the reader can live on another machine, and the classifiers can run in
their own container. What leaves your box in each mode, and what each mode loses in the cut, is
on [Running modes](../deploy/running-modes.md).

## Memory types

| Type | What it is |
|---|---|
| Year in review | a full year, every month keeping its share |
| Season | three months |
| Person spotlight | a year of one person, or the year ending on their birthday |
| Multi-person | a condition over named people: `"Riley" AND ("Casey" OR "Bob")` |
| Monthly highlights | one month, one minute |
| On this day | one date across the years |
| Album | one Immich album, nothing else |
| Trip | a GPS-detected trip, with the map |
| Holiday | the same holiday across several years |
| Surprise me | a day the library itself flagged, found by `discover-days` |

## The Memory page

One page: the **brief** (a type, its parameters, one duration), the **cut** (one row per phase
while it runs, with a bar and an estimate), and the result as a **storyboard** with the ranked
**story** one tab over. Tick or untick pictures in the pool and **Cut again**; then **Export**.

<ThemedScreenshot name="memory-brief" alt="The brief: memory type, its parameters and the duration line" />

The [CLI](../create/cli/generate.md) does the same job for scripts and schedules, and `runs story`
prints the storyboard in the terminal.

Stand it up with the [self-hosting guide](../deploy/self-hosting.md), then cut one month with
[Your first memory](../create/first-memory.mdx).

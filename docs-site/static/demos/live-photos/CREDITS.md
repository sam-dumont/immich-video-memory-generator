# Live Photo demo clips

`bike_race/`, `italian_hilltop/` and `landscape/` are the project author's own footage, published with
permission. They are the Live Photo demo on the docs page "Photos, Live Photos and HDR". They are not
covered by a stock licence and may not be reused outside this project's documentation.

`scripts/align_live_demo.py` rebuilds a `merged.mp4` from its `source_*.mp4` clips.
